## Brand & Style

This design system expresses a fusion of ultra-premium hospitality and high-precision clinical excellence. Tailored for an elite demographic in Riyadh, Saudi Arabia, the interface rejects sterile, utilitarian healthcare clichés in favor of warm architectural serenity, quiet confidence, and tactile digital luxury.

### Aesthetic Persona
- **Architectural Minimalism meets Modern Wellness**: Spatial generosity, refined micro-borders, and deliberate vertical rhythm evoke the physical sensation of stepping into an exclusive private clinic in Riyadh’s diplomatic quarter.
- **Bilingual Fluidity & Native RTL Sophistication**: Built from the ground up for right-to-left (Arabic) primacy with seamless left-to-right (English) parity. Typographic rhythm respects the natural calligraphic flow of Arabic script without artificial compression or distortion.
- **Discreet Prestige**: Authority is signaled through deep jewel-toned purples, muted slate inks, and precise luminous accents, rather than aggressive promotional devices.

### Design Movement
- **Refined Neo-Clinical**: Combining luminous, pristine clinical surfaces (`#FAF9FD`, `#FFFFFF`) with rich, imperial violet backdrops (`#38155E`), punctuated by micro-prismatic refraction accents (emerald, amber, magenta) that subtly reference modern light-curing and diagnostic laser optics.

## Colors

The color palette establishes an uncompromising sense of clinical precision tempered by bespoke luxury.

### Core Swatches
- **Primary (Imperial Violet)**:
  - Deep Anchor: `#280D45` (Primary containers, hero surfaces, high-contrast badges)
  - Core Brand: `#38155E` (Primary actions, main titles, focal brand moments)
  - Vivid Accent: `#4A1D7A` (Hover states, interactive elements)
- **Secondary (Luminous Azure)**:
  - Core Azure: `#0EA5E9` (Interactive links, active states, technology & precision badges)
  - Radiant Cyan: `#38BDF8` (Glows, focused rings, micro-indicators)
- **Clinical Prismatic Accents (Micro-Doses Only)**:
  - Healing Emerald: `#10B981` (Confirmed bookings, healthy diagnostics, clinical safety)
  - Warm Amber: `#F59E0B` (VIP tiers, cosmetic dentistry, gentle alerts)
  - Orchid Magenta: `#D946EF` (Pediatric, orthodontics, specialty procedures)
- **Surfaces & Atmosphere**:
  - App Canvas: `#FAF9FD` (Warm porcelain white)
  - Card & Modal Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pristine clinical plane)
  - Subtle Inset Surface: `#F3F0F9` (Search bars, inactive tabs, pill tracks)
- **Typography & Boundaries**:
  - Ink Headline: `#1E1B2E` (Near-charcoal with deep violet undertones)
  - Ink Body: `#4B465C` (Mid-tone slate violet for effortless readability)
  - Ink Subdued: `#76708A` (Captions, timestamps, disabled markers)
  - Boundary Line: `#ECE9F3` (Hairline dividers, clean architectural borders)

### Usage Rules
1. Never combine more than one prismatic accent (emerald, amber, magenta) within the same card or component view.
2. The primary deep violet must dominate interactive weight; the cyan serves exclusively for clarity, active state feedback, and technology highlights.

## Typography

The typography strategy leverages **IBM Plex Sans** (paired natively with **IBM Plex Sans Arabic** in production). Its humanist structure balances rigorous medical authority with architectural modernism.

### RTL & Arabic Considerations
- Arabic text naturally presents a higher optical baseline. Heading heights are extended to accommodate diacritics and tall ascenders (مثل الألف واللام) without clipping.
- Tracking (`letter-spacing`) must remain neutral (`0em`) or slightly negative on Arabic headings; positive letter spacing degrades legibility in cursive Arabic scripts and should only be applied to Western text or Latin dental notation.
- Digits should adapt dynamically between Eastern Arabic Numerals (١ ٢ ٣) and Western numerals depending on patient settings, standardizing on Western numerals for appointment dates and pricing clarity.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt base grid with an uncompromising mobile-first orientation.

### Grid Architecture
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4-column fluid layout with `1.25rem` outer margins and `1rem` gutters. Primary actions sit sticky within the safe bottom zone for thumb accessibility.
- **Tablet (768px - 1024px)**: 8-column layout with `2rem` outer margins. Cards reflow into symmetrical twin pairings.
- **Desktop (> 1024px)**: 12-column grid constrained to a maximum content container width of `1280px` centered on canvas. Margins scale dynamically to frame the clinic's editorial narratives.

### Spacing Harmony
- Section separations require deliberate breathing room (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) to project unhurried luxury.
- Interactive controls and grouped doctor credentials adhere to compact, strictly bounded internals (`space-sm` to `space-md`) to ensure density where medical clarity is paramount.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is treated as architectural lighting in an ultra-clean clinical setting. Shadows are warm, deeply diffused, and tinged with the brand’s deep purple undertone rather than neutral gray.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas)**: Background `#FAF9FD`.
2. **Level 1 (Clinical Plane / Cards)**: `#FFFFFF` with a crisp structural hairline border `1px solid #ECE9F3`. No blur or shadow; pure graphic precision.
3. **Level 2 (Hover / Elevated Panels)**: `box-shadow: 0 8px 24px -4px rgba(40, 13, 69, 0.06), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(40, 13, 69, 0.03)`. Micro-tinted violet ambient dissipation.
4. **Level 3 (Overlays / Modals / Booking Trays)**: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -12px rgba(40, 13, 69, 0.14), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(40, 13, 69, 0.04)`.
5. **Level 4 (Floating Dock / Primary Action Ring)**: Glassmorphic backdrop blur (`backdrop-filter: blur(16px)`) with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.85)` surface and `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(56, 21, 94, 0.12)`.

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **2** (`0.5rem` base, `1rem` for medium elements/cards, `1.5rem` for large containers and modal panels), delivering a polished, organic aesthetic that mirrors organic contours while preserving surgical precision.

### Application Rules
- **Micro-Elements (Chips, Checkboxes, Micro-Badges)**: `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Interactive Targets (Buttons, Inputs, Selectors)**: `0.75rem` (12px) to `1rem` (16px).
- **Cards & Clinical Panels**: `1rem` (16px) or `1.25rem` (20px).
- **Pills**: Specific trust badges, status indicators, and category filters use fully circular radii (`rounded-full` / 9999px).

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary CTA**: Background `#38155E`, text `#FFFFFF`, height `52px` (mobile-optimized touch target), radius `12px`. Hover shifts to `#4A1D7A` with a soft azure micro-glow (`box-shadow: 0 4px 16px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.2)`).
- **Secondary (Clinical Tech)**: Background `transparent`, border `1.5px solid #0EA5E9`, text `#0EA5E9`. Hover fills `rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.06)`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost**: Text `#38155E`, padding `8px 16px`. Active state subtly highlights with `#F3F0F9`.

### 2. Chips & Filter Tags
- Height `36px`, rounded pill silhouette (`9999px`).
- **Default State**: Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #ECE9F3`, text `#4B465C`.
- **Selected State**: Deep purple `#38155E` fill, text `#FFFFFF`, subtle azure indicator dot (`6px`) placed preceding the label (on the right in RTL).

### 3. Medical Trust Cards
- Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface enclosed by a fine `1px solid #ECE9F3` boundary.
- Top corner houses a Doctor Accreditation Badge: warm amber star rating or emerald certified stamp (`#10B981`) rendered on a pale emerald wash (`#ECFDF5`).
- Doctor portrait is softly framed inside a `rounded-2xl` mask with a hairline ring.

### 4. Input Fields
- Height `52px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #ECE9F3`, text `#1E1B2E`.
- Label floats with high-legibility `#76708A`.
- Focus state invokes a crisp `1.5px` border in `#38155E` complemented by an exterior `0 0 0 3px rgba(14, 165, 233, 0.15)` focus ring.
- Error state shifts border to deep crimson with clear iconography aligned to the start (right edge in RTL).

### 5. Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Checkbox: `20px x 20px` with `6px` radius. Border `1.5px solid #76708A`. Checked state fills `#38155E` displaying a crisp white checkmark.
- Radio: `20px` diameter with an inner concentric dot (`8px`) in `#0EA5E9` when selected.

### 6. Specialty Badges (Luxury & Prismatic Accents)
- **VIP Lounge / Concierge**: Background `linear-gradient(135deg, #280D45 0%, #4A1D7A 100%)`, gold/amber foil typography `#F59E0B`, border `1px solid rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.3)`.
- **Tooth Diagnostic Pill**: Ultra-fine pills denoting specific clinical procedures (e.g., "Orthodontics", "Laser Whitening") using a subtle `#FAF9FD` base, violet text, and a distinct 3px colored accent line along the leading edge (Emerald, Amber, or Cyan).
